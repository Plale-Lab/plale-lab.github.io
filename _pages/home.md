Plale Lab is an academic research lab at the University of Oregon carrying out research in computational and data infrastructure for AI, high performance and distributed computing, open science, provenance and reproducibility, and AI ethics and accountability.
We build systems &mdash; like the [Patra](https://github.com/plale-lab/patra-toolkit) model card toolkit and knowledge base and the [Cyberinfrastructure Knowledge Network (CKN)](https://github.com/plale-lab/cyberinfrastructure-knowledge-network) &mdash; that make AI/ML models and the infrastructure they run on more transparent, traceable, and accountable across their full lifecycle, from training to edge deployment.
