## Research

Plale Lab carries out research in computational and data infrastructure for AI, high performance and distributed computing, open science, provenance and reproducibility, and AI ethics and accountability.

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Computational &amp; Data Infrastructure for AI</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We design and build cyberinfrastructure that makes AI/ML models easier to train, document, deploy, and govern &mdash; including the Patra model card toolkit and knowledge base, which captures the full lifecycle of an AI/ML model from creation through deployment.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">High Performance &amp; Distributed Computing</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Our systems span the edge-cloud-HPC continuum, harmonizing resource-limited edge devices with centralized HPC and cloud resources &mdash; as in the Cyberinfrastructure Knowledge Network (CKN), an edge AI distributed framework built as part of the NSF ICICLE AI Institute.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Open Science</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We build open, reusable tools and knowledge infrastructure for the broader research community, contributing to consortia such as the Research Data Alliance and publishing our frameworks as open-source software.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Provenance &amp; Reproducibility</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We use provenance-based knowledge graphs to track how AI/ML models, datasets, and deployments relate to one another over time, enabling forward and backward provenance queries that support reproducibility and trust.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">AI Ethics &amp; Accountability</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We research semi-automated ways to capture fairness, explainability, and behavioral information about deployed AI/ML models, so that accountability doesn't depend solely on manual, one-time documentation.</p>
</div>
</div>

</div>
